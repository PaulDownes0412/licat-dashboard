About this data: OSFI's LICAT (Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test) framework requires life insurers to maintain adequate capital. This dataset reports contractual service margin (CSM) adjustments to available capital: D2040010025 — CSM reported as liabilities (added to retained earnings) and D2040010015 — CSM reported as assets (deducted from retained earnings). Values are in CAD $thousands. Source: OSFI Open Government Dataset
